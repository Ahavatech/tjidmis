About JIDMIS

The Journal of Intelligent Decision Making and Information Science is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to advancing research at the intersection of decision sciences, artificial intelligence, and information systems. The journal aims to be a leading forum for researchers, practitioners, and academics who are engaged in developing innovative methodologies, algorithms, and applications that enhance decision-making processes and improve information management across diverse fields.
